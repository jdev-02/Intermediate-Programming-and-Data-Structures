John Rolfe
One of the main challenges we faced in implementing our stock project was balancing all the ideas we initially had with the need to control scope and deliver something that actually worked. At the start, we brainstormed a lot of ambitious features—like supporting multiple types of financial instruments, pulling live data from multiple APIs, and adding advanced analysis methods—but it quickly became clear that trying to do everything would leave us with an incomplete or unstable product. We had to make deliberate choices about what to keep and what to scale back, such as focusing on equities instead of every financial instrument, and limiting API calls to reliable and accessible endpoints, since chasing every single edge case with API implementation quickly became impractical.
